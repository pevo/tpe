The trip at a glance

One loop around a small, wildly varied island

A deliberate rhythm for August heat and three generations: out by 8–8:30am, then A/C or a horizontal hour from 1–4pm (grandma's rest, the boys' pool block), then back out after 5pm for markets and golden-hour magic. Taipei days are intentionally swappable around weather; the east-coast festival days are the heart of the trip.

Before you go

To-dos, things to prepare & what to expect

✈️ Pack & prepare at home

  • International Driving Permits from AAA for both adults (needed for the Taitung rental car).
  • Travel insurance with typhoon & trip-interruption cover — August is peak typhoon season.
  • eSIMs for both adult phones, activated on landing.
  • 5 swim caps — mandatory at Taiwanese public pools and at Tien Lai's baths.
  • Water shoes ×5 — Cijin's black sand gets scorching.
  • Rash guards & sun hats · picnic mat (Luye + night glow) · motion-sickness bands (Yangmingshan road) · insect repellent (Luye at dusk) · portable fans.

🗓️ Expect & plan around

  • 5am jet-lag wake-ups the first days — lean in with dawn strolls and early breakfast lines.
  • The 1–4pm indoor block is non-negotiable in the heat, for grandma and honestly for everyone.
  • Night markets are cash — keep NT$2,000–3,000 in small bills; 7-Eleven ATMs take US cards; no tipping anywhere.
  • EasyCards for everyone at the airport/convenience store (~NT$500 loaded); under-6s ride the MRT free.
  • Typhoon flexibility: if a storm threatens the east coast Aug 11–14, re-sequence (Kaohsiung first, Taitung after) — flights & TRA rebook generously.
  • Balloon rides are weather-dependent — frame the tethered ride as a bonus, not a promise.

Good to know

Practical notes

☀️ Heat

90s°F and humid with afternoon thunderstorms. The 1–4pm indoor block is non-negotiable for grandma and honestly for everyone.

🌀 Typhoons

August is peak season. The festival pauses for weather; Taipei days are deliberately swappable; insurance covers the rest. If a storm threatens the east coast Aug 11–14, re-sequence (Kaohsiung first, Taitung after) — flights & TRA rebook generously.

💵 Money

Night markets are cash — keep NT$2,000–3,000 in small bills on hand. 7-Eleven ATMs take US cards. No tipping anywhere.

🚇 Getting around Taipei

The MRT is elevator-equipped and stroller/senior-friendly everywhere; never more than a NT$200 taxi if legs give out.

🎒 Pack

Swim caps ×5 · water shoes · rash guards & sun hats · picnic mat (Luye + night glow) · motion-sickness bands for the Yangmingshan road · insect repellent for Luye at dusk · portable fans.

🧮 Rough cost envelope (land only, family of five)

Hotels ~US$3,500–5,000 · TSA→TTT flights ~$350 · trains + HSR + metro ~$300 · Leofoo ~$450 · Taitung car + fuel ~$250 · vans/transfers ~$280 · balloon extras ~$90. Ballpark: US$5,200–6,700, before food, souvenirs & international airfare.
